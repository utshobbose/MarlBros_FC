# MarlBros FC — Phase 1 Features Specification
**Version:** 1.0 (Phase 1 Complete)  
**Date:** September 2026  
**Status:** Implemented & Verified  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 1 establishes MarlBros FC's brand identity online and automates the internal collection of the first **15 bespoke jerseys** for the 30-member squad. The application is built with a confident, editorial visual language inspired by *The Hollywood Reporter* (THR), combined with athletic discipline, a live interactive jersey customizer, friction-free manual bKash & WhatsApp checkout, and a password-gated administrative ledger with CSV export for screen printers.

---

## 2. Features Implemented in Phase 1

### 2.1 Editorial Visual Design & Branding
- **Monochrome & Club Palette:**
  - Strict high-contrast black (`#111111`, `#0A0A0A`), crisp white (`#FFFFFF`), newsprint off-white (`#FAF6EE`, `#F7F7F8`), and mid-grey metadata tones (`#6B6B6B`).
  - Subtle club crest colors used exclusively for brand authenticity: Deep Crimson (`#70111A`) and Vintage Ivory/Cream (`#F3EBDD`, `#FAF6EE`).
- **Typography:**
  - Bold, tight-tracked serif display headlines (`Playfair Display`).
  - Clean Swiss grotesque sans-serif (`Inter`) for body text and legible UI labels.
  - Condensed collegiate athletic digits (`Oswald`) for jersey back numbering.
  - All-caps category kickers (`editorial-kicker`) with generous tracking (`0.18em`).
- **Hollywood Reporter Layout Patterns:**
  - Sticky top header with slim top utility strip (live batch counter, WhatsApp desk, admin portal link).
  - Editorial lead stories with kicker badges and high-contrast typography.
  - Asymmetric card grids with hairline dividers instead of generic boxed cards.
  - "Squad Highlights / Club Dispatch" sidebar rail styled after THR's "Most Popular" rail.
  - Dense, multi-column dark footer with club heritage notes and payment coordination desks.

### 2.2 Official Team Jersey & Embroidered Crest
- **Crest Monogram (`CrestLogo` Component):**
  - High-fidelity vector SVG and high-resolution photographic embroidery patch variants.
  - Features the 5-point imperial crown, arched base, and interlocking "M" and "B" monogram with decorative serifs and flourish brackets.
  - Integrated into header masthead, hero module, jersey preview canvas, and footer.
- **Jersey Garment Details:**
  - Sourced directly from the official 2026 sample: deep crimson jacquard body, vintage cream polo collar with throat stripe inset, twin shoulder raglan stripes, curved breathable mesh flank panels, and sleeve cuffs.

### 2.3 Interactive Live Jersey Customizer (`/jersey`)
- **Real-Time 2D Simulator:**
  - Front and Back view toggles.
  - **Live Name Updating:** Member types their name (up to 12 uppercase characters) and it renders live on the jersey back in authentic serif arched lettering.
  - **Live Number Updating:** Member enters their squad number (0–99) and it renders live in double-stroke athletic collegiate digits.
  - **Size Selector:** Instant toggle between S, M, L, XL, and XXL with a dedicated Sizing Guide drawer (Chest, Length, and Shoulder inches).
  - **Quantity Selector:** Increment/decrement quantity with live total pricing calculation in Bangladeshi Taka (`৳850` per unit).
  - Mode toggle between interactive SVG simulator and official studio photography.
  - "Proceed to Order" button directly pre-fills the checkout form via URL search parameters.

### 2.4 Frictionless Member Checkout (`/order`)
- **Fields Collected:**
  - Member Full Name (required)
  - Phone Number (Bangladeshi 11-digit validation)
  - Jersey Size (S / M / L / XL / XXL)
  - Name on Jersey (max 12 characters, uppercase enforcement)
  - Jersey Number (0–99)
  - Quantity (default 1, max 15)
  - Special fitting / delivery notes (optional)
- **Zero-Friction Submission:**
  - Generates unique reference code (e.g. `MBFC-2026-XXXX`).
  - Saves to database (`orders` table via Supabase or resilient local cache).

### 2.5 Instant Confirmation Screen (`OrderConfirmation`)
- **Itemized Order Summary:** Item, size, custom name, number, quantity, and total payable amount.
- **bKash Send Money Box:**
  - Prominent display of club bKash number (`01712-345678`).
  - One-click "Copy Number" button with visual feedback.
  - Clear instructions: Send Money with Order Reference or Name as reference.
- **WhatsApp Deep-Link CTA Button:**
  - High-visibility green WhatsApp button.
  - Links to `https://wa.me/8801XXXXXXXXX?text=...` with pre-filled message containing order ref, member name, size, custom name, and number for 1-tap confirmation.
- **Receipt Print Option:** Built-in clean printable receipt trigger.

### 2.6 Password-Gated Administrative Portal (`/admin`)
- **Security:** Protected by passcode (default: `marlbros2026`, configurable in `.env`).
- **Live Statistics Strip:**
  - Total Orders Count
  - Total Jerseys Ordered (vs. 15 unit internal limit)
  - Pending bKash Payments
  - Paid & Verified Count
  - Fulfilled / Printed Count
  - Total Revenue Booked (BDT)
- **Order Management Table:**
  - Full display of all orders with timestamps, member names, custom print details, phone numbers, and notes.
  - **1-Click Status Toggling:** Switch orders between `Pending Payment`, `Paid`, `Fulfilled`, or `Cancelled`.
  - **Direct WhatsApp Chat Link:** Next to each member's order to follow up or confirm receipt in 1 click.
  - Real-time search query filter (by name, phone, order ref, or custom name) and status filter dropdown.
- **Manufacturer CSV Export:**
  - Generates print-ready `.csv` file formatted for the screen printer containing Order Reference, Member Name, Phone, Size, Jersey Name, Jersey Number, Quantity, and Status.

### 2.7 Club Identity & Heritage Page (`/about`)
- **The 7 to 30 Story:** Deep chronological narrative of how 7 founding friends in 2023 expanded into a 30-member competitive football fraternity.
- **Heraldic Crest Breakdown:** Visual explainer detailing the Imperial Crown, the Interlocking MB Monogram, and the Crimson & Cream color philosophy.
- **Complete Squad Roster (30 Members):**
  - Categorized into Goalkeepers, Defenders, Midfielders, Forwards, and Coaching Staff.
  - Highlighting the Founding Seven members with special `★ Founder` badges.

### 2.8 Photographic Archive & Lightbox (`/gallery`)
- Filterable gallery grid with categories: "All", "Reveal", "Matches", "Training", "Brotherhood".
- Keyboard-accessible full-screen Lightbox with Escape to close, Left/Right arrow navigation, image zoom, and photo captions.

### 2.9 Communication & FAQ Desk (`/contact`)
- Club leadership contacts.
- Matchday and training schedule (Victoria Stadium / Green Turf).
- Interactive FAQ accordion answering questions regarding bKash verification, custom printing limits, delivery timelines, and Phase 2 public orders.
